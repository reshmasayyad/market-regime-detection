# Market Regime Detection

Compare simple risk-and-trend rules, K-means, and Gaussian Hidden Markov Models on NIFTY 50, NIFTY BANK and India VIX. Use chronological selection, causal filtering for held-out dates, and explicit robustness diagnostics.

Market states are statistical descriptions, not authoritative labels. The project evaluates density fit, persistence, initialization sensitivity, and subsequent observed volatility without claiming supervised accuracy or trading profitability.
